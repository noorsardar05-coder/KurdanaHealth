# BodyWise anatomical atlas

Models from **Z-Anatomy** (https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy), derived from **BodyParts3D**
(Database Center for Life Science, Japan).

License: **CC BY-SA 4.0**

Files in `atlas/`:

- `visceral.fbx` — visceral organs (lungs, liver, stomach, kidneys, pancreas, intestines, bladder, …)
- `lymphoid.fbx` — spleen and lymphoid structures
- `skeleton.fbx` — skeletal system
- `muscles.fbx` — muscular system
- `cardiovascular.fbx` — heart and vessels
- `nervous.fbx` — brain, spinal cord, peripheral nerves

BodyWise loads these as **visibility layers of one shared coordinate system**.
Organs are never placed by hand. Missing structures are hidden, not invented.
