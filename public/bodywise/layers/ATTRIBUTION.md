# BodyWise lightweight anatomy layers

## What this is

Original educational SVG diagrams created for **Kurdana Health / BodyWise (Medico2026)**.

They use a shared coordinate system:

- viewBox: `0 0 360 820`
- Anterior view facing the viewer
- Patient's right = image left (standard anatomical orientation)

Organ placement follows standard surface anatomy and abdominal quadrants
(e.g. liver in the upper-right quadrant, stomach upper-left, heart mediastinum
with apex slightly left).

## What this is not

- Not a scan or redistribution of Complete Anatomy, BioDigital, or Netter plates
- Not a replacement for clinical imaging or professional medical atlases
- Not derived from the heavy Z-Anatomy FBX default (that atlas remains optional Advanced 3D only)
